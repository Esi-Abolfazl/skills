## Caveats

- Cagan's "empowered teams" fits product companies better than agencies or government work with a fixed mandate; there the client's decisions bound the team.
- RICE's impact scale (3 / 2 / 1 / 0.5 / 0.25) is Intercom's own, not a standard.
- DHM, positioning and Lean Analytics assume a commercial product with competitors and margin; skip them for a mandated public service.
- Doshi's LNO and pre-mortem categories rest on secondary write-ups; his primary posts are on X.
